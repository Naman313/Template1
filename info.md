## 4. Programs (from Programs page)

- **General schedule:** "Open at 7:30 AM – Monday to Friday", availability "2-5 days per week". "Breakfast and Snacks Provided" though "Lunch (not provided)."

- **Infant Program** — serves children "one month to fifteen months." Designed to meet developmental needs with "individual schedules" and "consistent care" in a "safe, healthy and stimulating environment."

- **Toddler Program** — serves children "fifteen months to thirty-three months." Focuses on social, emotional, physical, and cognitive development in a "safe and caring environment."

- **Preschool Program** — serves children "2.9 – 5 years." Features "age appropriate curriculum" designed to prepare children for kindergarten, emphasizing "writing, letter and number recognition."

- **Non-discrimination policy:** "does not discriminate on the basis of race, religion, cultural heritage, national origin, political beliefs, marital status, sexual orientation or anyone with disabilities in providing services to children and their families."

- **Program features (site-wide):** Games ("Games in early childhood is the best foundation for success in school."), Activities ("Our activities will challenge and develop your child's creativity, imagination, thinking skills, and social skills."), Education ("Our center provides many educational activities to keep your kids engaged."), Learn through play, Education Services, Toys and Games, Child Care, Love and Care, Physical Activity, active and quiet play, outdoor activities, scheduled field trips throughout the school year.
